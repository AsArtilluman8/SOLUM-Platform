# Code of Conduct

SOLUM Platform aims to be a respectful open-source project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Give technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language.
- Spam or intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants who repeatedly violate these rules.
